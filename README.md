# Vina Skomina

Family winery and cellar in Oševljek, in the Lower Vipava Valley (Primorska, Slovenia).

**Website:** https://voixy25.github.io/VinaSkomina/

## About

Vina Skomina grows Malvazija, Rebula and Vitovska Grganja on two vineyards: one on the open, sunny slope below Oševljek, where the bora wind blows, and one tucked into the cooler, wooded ground higher up towards the Karst edge. The soil mixes flysch and limestone. Everything is harvested by hand.

The cellar makes a house white blend, sold by the litre, and cures its own meats: prosciutto, salami, pancetta and sweet sausages. Neapolitan pizza is baked fresh in a rotating electric oven for visitors.

## What the site offers

- **Wine and food:** the house white, the cured meats and the pizzas, each with a short description.
- **Tastings:** a cellar tasting of the white blend with a cold-cuts platter, or a longer visit that adds a walk through the vineyards and pizza baked on site.
- **Booking:** an enquiry form for tastings and orders, with date, group size and language. Groups of eight or more are asked to call. Replies within 24 hours; free cancellation up to 24 hours before the visit.
- **Terroir:** a map of the cellar and both vineyards, with links to Google Maps and Apple Maps.
- **FAQ:** parking, cancellation, cash, pets and weather.
